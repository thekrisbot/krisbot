NO WIN, NO THANK YOU

Have you been a victim of the British politeness? You could be eligible for a compensation...
November 11, 2026

FADE IN:

INT. CLAIMS OFFICE - DAY

TREVOR PRICE, a solicitor in a navy suit (40's), sits at a desk with a mug full of hot coffee.

TREVOR

Hello, I’m Trevor Price of Price, Liz, and Wright Solicitors. Have you been a victim of British Politeness? Maybe you were just going about your day, minding your own business, when somebody was so terribly polite to you that it affected your life, or the life of a loved one. If so, you could be entitled to compensation. Last year alone, over four million British residents were seriously inconvenienced by someone being polite. Let’s hear from some of them.

INT. COFFEE HOUSE - DAY

HELEN (30's) carefully balances a tray with hot coffee, sandwiches and napkins. She is about to sneeze, and the sneeze threatens to make her lose her balance and topple the tray. After holding it in for a few seconds, she sneezes and somehow keeps the tray balanced. Three OLD LADIES (60's) appear behind her and shout in unison.

OLD LADIES

(in unison)

Bless you.

(Helen is startled. The tray tips and the hot coffee spills over her dress, scalding her.)

EXT. OFFICE ENTRANCE - DAY

A MIDDLE-AGED MAN (50's) holds open a glass door. At the far end of a long paved walkway, several metres away, MARK (40's) carries a laptop bag. He gestures to the man to let the door go, but the man insists on holding it. Mark sprints toward the door at full speed and makes it.

MARK

Thanks so much, sorry about that, thank you.

MIDDLE-AGED MAN

Oh, that’s alright. Have a nice day, yeah?

Mark gets inside the building. A notification pops up on his smart watch: “Unusual Heart Rate. Calling Emergency Services.”

MARK

(nervously, looking at the watch)

Uh-oh.

Mark, completely out of breath, collapses to the floor.

INT. BUS - DAY

ROB (30's) sits in a priority seat. MRS CRAMPTON (70's) gets on the bus with a cane. Rob stands up and gestures to the seat. Mrs Crampton gestures back for him not to get up.

ROB

That’s alright, I’m getting off at the next stop anyway.

MRS CRAMPTON

Oh, how kind, but I am fine.

ROB

I insist.

MRS CRAMPTON

Oh, that is very sweet, but I am quite alright right here.

ROB

(slight change in tone)

Madam, I insist that you take this one.

MRS CRAMPTON

I insist more.

(A few minutes later, they are at each other’s throats, screaming and fighting. The last image is MRS CRAMPTON using her cane to do a fish hook.)

EXT. HIGH STREET - DAY

NEIL (30's), a gold chain around his neck, stands on the pavement. A CHAIN SNATCHER (20's) in a hoodie grabs the chain and sprints away. Neil shouts after him and gives chase. Further along the pavement, GEOFF (60's) stands beside a Lime bike at the kerb, looking at his phone. The Chain Snatcher slows down next to him, bent over, out of breath.

GEOFF

Oh, you alright, young man? Here, take my bike, I’ve only just unlocked it, but I’m in no rush, you see. I’m only going to the post office.

(In the background, Neil closes in, waving and shouting towards Geoff, who doesn’t notice. The Chain Snatcher shifts on his feet, listening to Geoff.)

GEOFF (CONT’D)

But I could use a walk, and you look like you need it more than I do. Here you go.

CHAIN SNATCHER

Cheers, mate.

(The Chain Snatcher gets on the bike and rides away. Geoff waves after him. Neil arrives, fuming, and stops beside Geoff.)

NEIL

What did you do that for?

GEOFF

That’s the British way, young man.

NEIL

(angry)

That was my bloody chain, you old git!!

INT. CLAIMS OFFICE - DAY

TREVOR sits at the desk with the mug in his hand. A graphic appears at the bottom of the screen:

Price, Liz, and Wright Solicitors
0808 157 0123
www.pricelizwright.co.uk
NO WIN, NO FEE.

TREVOR

If you or someone you know has been a victim of British Politeness, call Price, Liz, and Wright. Today.

LEGAL VOICE (V.O.)

Compensation is not available to anyone who has said thank you to the bus driver.

(Trevor sneezes. A SECRETARY (30's) appears beside him.)

SECRETARY

Bless you!

(Trevor is startled. He drops the mug on himself and screams.)

BLACKOUT.

END.
